The honest part

This is the pitching part of PR. The agent finds the right opportunities and writes the pitch. It doesn't write the content itself, so if an editor says yes to a contributed article, you or your team write it. It also won't send pitches for you, introduce you to journalists or promise coverage. What it gives you is a weekly, repeatable way to put the right ideas in front of the right editors, without an agency bill.
