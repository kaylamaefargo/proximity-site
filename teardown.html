The Revenue Chain Teardown

Your pipeline problem isn’t a pipeline problem.

Deals stall in the first call, so you hire a sales consultant to fix the discovery script. Nothing changes, because the real cause is upstream.

Book a discovery call See if it fits

Maybe the ads are bringing in people who were never going to buy. Maybe the site never gave anyone a reason to act this quarter instead of next one. Maybe your rep is trying to manufacture urgency from scratch on a thirty-minute call, because nothing before that call built any.

A marketing consultant can’t see how the call actually goes. A sales consultant can’t see what the ad promised. I trace the whole chain, first impression to closed-lost note, and tell you the one thing to fix first.

Built for companies with a real sales motion and a number they already don't like.

This fits if:

  • You have an established B2B sales motion — reps, a pipeline, a CRM with history in it.
  • There's a specific metric you already distrust: win rate, stage-one stall rate, Closed No Decision, or CAC payback.
  • You can pull four quarters of closed-won and closed-lost data, with stage history and loss reasons.
  • You suspect the problem isn't where everyone on the team has been looking — and you'd rather know for certain than keep guessing.

Run your own math: if a five-point improvement in win rate is worth more than $10,000 to your business over the next year — and for most B2B companies with a real deal size, it is — this is a rounding error against what the leak is already costing you.

Here’s what I’ll ask you for to run the diagnostic.

Send as much of this as you can before Day 1. It’s the raw material the Teardown gets built from, not paperwork, and almost no one has all of it.

Systems and materials

  • CRM access, or an export: the last four quarters of closed-won and closed-lost, with stage history and loss reasons
  • The outbound tools your reps use, and the sequences as they run today
  • Your call recording software, so I can choose which calls to listen to
  • Sales decks, one-pagers and battle cards
  • Your top five landing pages
  • Paid media and organic strategy documents

If you can’t easily produce the closed-lost data, that’s already a finding.

The people

I want to hear from a handful of the people closest to your revenue chain. We’ll decide exactly who at intake.

  • Sales reps
  • Marketing team members
  • Executives
  • Other key stakeholders

Async A short survey and a Loom from each person, by default.
Live A conversation only where it’s worth it, which we’ll decide together.

Same schema every time. Ranked. One "fix this first," then everything else below it.

Symptom
The number you already hate — win rate, stage-one stall rate, Closed No Decision, CAC payback.
Position
Where in the chain it actually originates — ad, landing page, discovery call, proposal — not just where it shows up.
Root Cause
The thing underneath it, once the evidence rules out the obvious explanations everyone already tried.
Evidence
What I actually saw — call recordings, CRM stage history, ad copy, page analytics — with receipts, not assumptions.
Fix
The specific move. Not a framework, not "align sales and marketing" — an action you could start this week.
Expected Lift
The honest range, based on your actual volume and deal size — not a promise.
